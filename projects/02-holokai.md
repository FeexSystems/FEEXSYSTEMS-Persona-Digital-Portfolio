## Overview

HoloKai explores a digital intelligence environment for cultural and civilization knowledge. Its direction combines structured knowledge, AI reasoning, artifact intelligence and immersive 3D experiences.

## Design philosophy

Cultural knowledge should not be reduced to disconnected pages. The system direction treats people, places, artifacts, events, traditions and relationships as entities that can be explored through a semantic world.
